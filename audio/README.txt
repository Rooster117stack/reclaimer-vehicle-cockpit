radio/*.wav: the four supplied MP3 songs decoded to stereo PCM16, 44100 Hz.

Add or replace radio stations using stereo PCM16 WAV at 44100 Hz, then restart the
helper. Stations are sorted by filename. F9 toggles radio; F10 cycles stations.
